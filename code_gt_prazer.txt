Different people get different amounts of pleasure from different things.

For instance, some people get lots of pleasure from food, whereas other people get lots of pleasure from art and entertainment, and other people get pleasure from caring for a pet or tending their garden.

We ran two studies on a total of 600 people in the U.S. asking them (1) how much pleasure they got from many different possible sources of pleasure and (2) to list other sources they found pleasure in that weren't included in our list.

Based on that study, we have created this assessment that aims to help you identify what *your greatest Sources of Pleasure in life are* and reflect on how to add more pleasure to your life. It will also help you see how similar and different you are to other people with regard to where your enjoyment comes from.
